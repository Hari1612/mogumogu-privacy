FAQ

  • A verdict looks wrong — tap "Why?" on the result to see the reasoning, and use the report option (or email us the product barcode + a label photo). Always trust the printed label over the app.
  • How do I add family members? — Home → family row → Add family.
  • How do I delete my data? — Settings → Sign out, then email us a deletion request; we remove your account, profiles, photos, and history.
  • Scan uses a credit? — each AI label scan uses one free credit; barcode lookups of known products are free.

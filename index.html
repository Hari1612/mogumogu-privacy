MoguMogu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03 · Operator: Sapporosoft (Sapporo, Japan) · Contact: info.hari07@gmail.com

What we collect and why

  • Account email — to create and secure your account.
  • Dietary & allergy profiles (for you and family members you add) — used solely to compute product verdicts. This is sensitive health-related information; we treat it accordingly. Family-member profiles are entered and controlled by the account holder.
  • Scan photos — label photos you capture or upload are sent to our server and processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) to read ingredients and allergens. Photos are used only to produce your scan result.
  • Scan history — products you scanned and the verdicts shown, so you can review them.
  • Approximate (coarse) location — optional for in-store features. Precise delivery destinations are handled separately when you choose delivery, as described below.
  • Technical diagnostics — anonymous scan-pipeline events identified only by a random per-scan trace ID. No name, email, or device identity is attached. Retained up to 14 days.

Shop orders and delivery location

When you order from a participating MoguMogu-verified shop, we store the items, quantities, prices, ordering mode, table or seat if applicable, order and payment status, and order timestamps. Payment is made to shop staff on delivery or at the shop; this ordering feature does not collect card or bank details.

Your name, contact phone, delivery address, building/floor/unit, confirmed destination pin and any requests you enter are shared with the shop’s authorized active staff to fulfil and support that order. Your private dietary and family profiles are not automatically sent to the shop. Requests you choose to write may contain allergy information. You can enter a delivery pin manually; your phone’s precise GPS is requested only when you tap the location action.

Assigned shop delivery staff may explicitly share their precise GPS during an active delivery. The customer who placed that order can see the latest position, accuracy and update time. This is foreground sharing: updates may pause if the staff phone is locked or the page is closed. Sharing ends on delivery completion, cancellation or explicit stop. We keep only the latest point, not a route history; stale points are routinely deleted after 10 minutes without an update. GPS is not used for advertising.

Contact, delivery and request details are encrypted at rest and normally removed 30 days after an order closes. Unresolved fulfilment, payment collection or refunds can require longer retention. Order items, amounts, status changes and cash audit records remain as transaction records. Account deletion removes account linkage and closed-order personal details; details needed for unresolved fulfilment, payment collection or refunds may remain until resolution. An interrupted checkout may be saved in account-scoped secure storage on your device solely to retry the same submission without duplication, and is cleared when its result is confirmed or you sign out.

Saved checkout details. When you choose “Remember for next time,” we separately save your name, phone and selected delivery location to your account. You can save multiple named locations (for example Home or Office), choose a default, edit them or remove them in Saved details. These reusable contact and location records are encrypted at rest and remain until you remove them or delete your account; the 30-day order-detail retention above does not apply to your saved address book. A shop receives only the details you use for its order, not your other locations.

Product ratings. After an order is served, delivered or collected, you may rate each purchased product from 1 to 5 stars. A rating can be edited, and each completed purchase contributes once. Public menus display anonymous averages and counts; your name, contact and address are not shown with ratings. Ratings are removed when you delete your account. Customer ratings are not food-safety assessments.

Anonymous return-visit statistics

To learn whether MoguMogu is useful, the app sends at most one tiny anonymous message per day, only on days you scan something. The entire message is two dates: the date of your first scan, and today's date. For example: {"cohort_day":"2026-08-01","active_day":"2026-08-08"}. It contains no account, no name, no device identifier, no location, and nothing about what you scanned. It is sent without any login credentials, and our server does not store your IP address for these messages — only running totals per date pair are kept. After 45 days of use, the app stops sending these messages entirely. We use the counts to answer one question: do people who scan once come back and scan again?

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → "Share anonymous return-visit statistics." Turning it off takes effect immediately. There is nothing to delete afterwards, because no stored record can be connected to you or your device.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale of personal data. No sharing of personal data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No rotating device identifiers or fingerprinting.

Service providers (processors)

  • Anthropic — AI processing of label photos and text (ingredient reading).
  • Hosting — our servers are hosted in Japan (Sakura Internet).
  • Apple / Google — app distribution and standard platform services.

These providers process data only on our instructions to run the service.

Aggregated statistics (future)

We may share fully anonymous, aggregated product-level statistics (for example, how many times a product was scanned overall) with food companies or researchers. Such statistics never include your identity, profile, photos, or any information about you.

Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your account and data at any time via info.hari07@gmail.com. We comply with Japan's APPI and honor equivalent rights (access, correction, deletion) for users elsewhere, including the EU.

Retention

Account data and scan history: until you delete your account. Diagnostics: up to 14 days. Scan photos: retained to provide your results and improve reading accuracy; deleted upon account deletion request.

Children

MoguMogu accounts are for adults. Parents may add family-member profiles (including children) under their own account; that information is provided and controlled by the parent.

Important safety note

Verdicts are informational only and may contain errors. Always check the product label yourself, especially for severe allergies.

Changes

We will post updates to this page. Material changes will be noted in-app.
